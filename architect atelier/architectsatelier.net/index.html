About Architects Atelier

Ar. Gaurav Saini, co-founder and partner of ARCHITECTS ATELIER , has been practicing in Greater Noida since 2005, alongside Ar. Mamta Saini.

With a Bachelor's degree from GCA, Lucknow, U.P., a Master's in Ekistics from Jamia Millia Islamia, New Delhi, and a second Master's in Town & Country Planning from the Institute of Town Planners India, New Delhi.

ARCHITECTS ATELIER deals with various project types including Healthcare, Institutional, Commercial, Hospitality, and High-End Residential projects.

His diverse educational background and extensive experience make him a valuable asset in the field of Architecture and Planning.
